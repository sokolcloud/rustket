## Who to ask

- Problems with the Rustket app itself (crashes, display problems, a
  feature that does not work as described): contact us.
- Your account, password, rooms, permissions or messages: these are managed
  by your workspace's administrators. Ask them; we have no access to
  workspaces.
- Objectionable content or abusive users: report them in the app (see
  below). Reports go to the workspace's moderators, who can remove content
  and accounts.

## Safety tools in Rustket

- Report a message: right-click a message, then Report.
- Report a user: click the user's avatar (User info), then Report user (also
  in a message's menu).
- Block a user: open a direct message, click the User info (i) button at the
  top right, then Block.
  Their messages are hidden and neither of you can write in that
  conversation.
- Ignore a user in a room: click the user's avatar (User info), then Ignore.
- Hidden words: Settings, Privacy & storage, Hidden words.
- Delete your account (when the workspace allows it): My account, Security,
  Delete my account.

## Common questions

**Do I need an account with you?** No. You sign in to your organization's
or community's chat server. Enter its address on Rustket's first screen.

**Where is my data?** On the chat servers you add, and in caches and the
macOS keychain on your Mac. We do not collect anything. See the
[privacy policy](https://sokolcloud.github.io/rustket/privacy/).

**How do I update Rustket?** The Mac App Store version updates through the
App Store.
