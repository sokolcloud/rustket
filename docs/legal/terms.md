## 4. Reporting and blocking

Rustket gives you tools to deal with unwanted content:

- Report message: right-click a message, then Report.
- Report user: open a user's profile (or a message menu), then Report user.
- Block: in a direct message, open the user's profile, then Block. Their
  messages are hidden and neither of you can write in that conversation.
- Ignore: in a room, open a user's profile, then Ignore.
- Hidden words: Settings, Privacy & storage, Hidden words collapses
  messages containing words you choose.

Reports go to the moderators of the workspace where the content was posted.
They review reports and can remove content and suspend or remove accounts.
If a workspace does not act on serious abuse, contact its operator or the
appropriate authorities. You can also contact us (section 8); we cannot act
on a workspace's content, but we will help you find the right contact.
