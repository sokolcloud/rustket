# Rustket privacy policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026.

## The short version

- We do not run chat servers for Rustket users.
- We do not collect, receive or store any of your data.
- Rustket has no analytics, telemetry, advertising or crash reporting.
- Your data goes only to the chat servers (workspaces) you add yourself.

## How Rustket works

Rustket is a client for team chat servers that run the Rocket.Chat server
software. Each server is operated by an organization or community (for
example your employer), not by us. When you add a server and sign in,
Rustket talks directly to that server: your messages, files, profile,
contacts and presence go to it and come from it. What that server does with
your data is governed by its operator's own privacy policy and terms. Ask
your workspace's administrators about them.

We have no access to the servers you use, to your account on them, or to
anything you send or receive.

## Data stored on your device

Rustket keeps some data on your computer so it can work:

- Settings: the servers you added, your preferences, the hidden words you
  chose and similar options, in Rustket's configuration folder.
- Sign-in tokens and end-to-end encryption keys: in the system's secure
  storage (the macOS keychain; on other systems the system keyring). Where
  no keyring is available, builds outside the Mac App Store (Linux, Windows
  and the downloadable macOS build) keep them in a file in Rustket's data
  folder in your user profile, unless you choose to keep them in memory only
  (Settings, Privacy & storage). The Mac App Store version keeps them in
  memory only in that case.
- Caches: images, avatars and recent messages, so the app opens quickly
  and works briefly offline. You can clear them in Settings, Privacy &
  storage.

This data never leaves your device except to the server it belongs to. If
you uninstall Rustket, delete its folders to remove it; removing a server
in Rustket deletes its sign-in token.

## Connections Rustket makes

- To the servers you add, and to services those servers point to (for
  example file storage or a single sign-on provider of your organization).
- To your web browser, when you open a link, sign in with an external
  provider, or join a video call. The website you open then has its own
  privacy policy.
- To the websites that images and link previews in messages come from, when
  Rustket shows them; those sites see your IP address.
- Builds downloaded outside the Mac App Store can check GitHub for new
  versions when automatic update checks are on; GitHub sees only your IP
  address, the app version and the operating system. The Mac App Store
  version makes no such request; updates come from the App Store.

## Children

Rustket is a tool for workplaces and communities and is not directed at
children. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

## Your rights

Because we do not hold any personal data about you, there is nothing for us
to show, correct or delete. For data held by a chat server, contact that
server's operator. If the server allows it, you can delete your account
there from Rustket: My account, Security, Delete my account.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will publish the new version on this page with a
new date. A change that affects how your data is handled will also be
announced in the app's release notes.
